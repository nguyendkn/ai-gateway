# CRO Framework

Use these checks to diagnose conversion issues and plan tests.

## Core Checks

1. Headline 4-U: useful, unique, urgent, ultra-specific.
2. Above fold: problem focus, no company story, value visible without scroll.
3. CTA first person: prefer "Get My Guide" over "Get Your Guide" when natural.
4. Form friction: max 5 fields. Progressive profile rest.
5. Message match: ad promise, page headline, CTA, offer must align.
6. Social proof: near CTAs; faces/names/results beat logos alone.
7. Bias stack: loss aversion, social proof, anchoring.
8. PAS copy: problem, agitate, solve. Emotion before logic.
9. Urgency: only real deadlines/limits. No fake timers.
10. Price anchoring: show expensive option first when pricing is relevant.
11. Trust cluster: security, guarantees, policies grouped near risk points.
12. F-pattern: put headline, proof, CTA in scan path.
13. Lead magnet hierarchy: templates > checklists > guides.
14. Objection preemption: answer top 3 concerns before CTA or near FAQ.
15. Mobile thumb zone: primary CTAs reachable with thumb.
16. One-variable tests: isolate change, measure lift, compound wins.
17. Post-conversion momentum: thank-you page sells next step.
18. Cart recovery: email 1h, retarget 4h, incentive 24h.
19. Reading level: grade 6, short words, 11-word sentences.
20. Funnel stage: TOFU education, MOFU comparison, BOFU decision.
21. White space: focus. Crowded page = confused choice.
22. Benefit first: feature says what; benefit says why; transformation sells.
23. Micro-commitment: small yes before big yes; start with email when possible.
24. Tracking stack: heatmaps show problem; recordings show why; events show what.
25. Weekly ritual: Monday metrics, Tuesday test, iterate or scale.

## Priority Model

Rank findings:

- P0: broken promise, broken flow, invisible CTA, unreadable mobile, form failure.
- P1: weak above-fold value prop, high-friction form, missing proof/trust, unclear offer.
- P2: copy polish, hierarchy tweaks, non-critical proof, secondary objections.
- P3: nice-to-have experiments with weak evidence.

## Recommended Plan Phases

- Phase 01: Evidence and funnel diagnosis.
- Phase 02: Above-fold, offer, headline, CTA.
- Phase 03: Form, trust, proof, objections.
- Phase 04: Mobile, performance, tracking, experiment setup.
- Phase 05: Post-conversion and recovery flows, when relevant.
